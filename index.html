About

Pavlo Tyshkovets

I'm a frontend developer with three and a half years of commercial experience, and my specialism is real-time 3D product configurators — the kind where a customer sizes a product, changes its finish, and sees the result before they buy. Several are in production.

Most of that work is Three.js, React and TypeScript. Around it I've shipped a Phaser game inside a Telegram Mini App, an app for Samsung Tizen televisions, Next.js storefronts, and 2D canvas work in Fabric.js. I own deployments end to end: Linux, nginx, DNS, CDN, rsync.

The demos here are modified builds. Client branding and proprietary data are gone and backends are replaced with local fixtures — each one says so in the corner and explains exactly what changed. The engineering is untouched.

Built by Pavlo Tyshkovets. No analytics, no cookies, nothing to consent to. tishkovets.pavlo@gmail.com

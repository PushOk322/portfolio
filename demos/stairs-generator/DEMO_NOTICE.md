## Specific to this demo

**Stairs Generator** — a procedural staircase configurator. Built by
Pavlo Tyshkovets at **Marevo Vision** as internal R&D. Published here as a
showcase of the work, not as a product.

There was **no client brand and no backend to remove** from this one. It ships as
built, with three changes:

- Model textures re-encoded to WebP (7.13 MB → 1.26 MB) so it loads fast on a phone.
- A dismissible "Portfolio build" badge added in the corner.
- Agent-workflow directories (`.superpowers/`, `.claude/`, `docs/superpowers/`)
  left out of the copy.

The badge deliberately does **not** say "mock data" — this demo has none, and never
did. Nothing in it is faked.
